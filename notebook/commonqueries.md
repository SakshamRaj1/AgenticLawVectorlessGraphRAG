**Police Action, Arrest, FIR & Bail (BNSS 2023 + Constitution Part III)**

* **Refusal to register an FIR across jurisdictions:** "I lost my phone and wallet on a train while traveling from Bihar to Delhi, and my local police station is refusing to register an FIR saying the incident happened outside their jurisdiction. What does the law require them to do?" *(Tests BNSS § 173 Zero FIR / e-FIR)*
* **Arrest without notice in minor offences:** "Police came to my house without a warrant over a dispute carrying a maximum punishment of 2 years and threatened immediate arrest. Can they arrest me without issuing a prior notice to appear?" *(Tests BNSS § 35(3), Constitution Art. 21)*
* **Illegal detention & denial of lawyer:** "My brother was picked up by police yesterday morning, neither our family was informed nor has he been produced before a Magistrate even after 30 hours, and they refused to let his advocate meet him during questioning. Which constitutional and procedural rights are violated?" *(Tests Constitution Art. 22(1)–(2), BNSS §§ 38, 47, 48, 57)*
* **Protection before arrest:** "A business rival has threatened to file a false non-bailable criminal case against me. How can I apply for protection from arrest before an FIR is registered?" *(Tests BNSS § 482 Anticipatory bail)*
* **Prolonged undertrial detention:** "An accused person has already spent half of the maximum imprisonment period in jail while waiting for his trial to finish. Is he entitled to release on bail?" *(Tests BNSS § 479)*

**Everyday Criminal Offences & Self-Defence (BNS 2023)**

* **Chain/phone snatching vs. theft:** "Two men on a motorcycle snatched my mobile phone from my hand while I was walking on the street and sped away without hurting me. Is this ordinary theft or a separate offence under BNS 2023, and what is the punishment?" *(Tests BNS § 304 Snatching vs. § 303 Theft)*
* **Hit-and-run road accident:** "A speeding driver hit a pedestrian at night and fled the spot without reporting the accident to the police or a Magistrate. What criminal liability and punishment apply under BNS 2023?" *(Tests BNS §§ 281, 106(2))*
* **Self-defence against an armed intruder:** "An intruder broke into my home at night armed with a knife, and while defending myself I caused fatal injuries to him. Under what circumstances does the right of private defence extend to causing death?" *(Tests BNS §§ 37, 38, 41)*
* **Advance payment fraud vs. employee theft:** "A supplier took an advance payment promising to deliver goods and vanished, while a separate staff member ran away with company laptops entrusted to him. How does BNS distinguish these two offences?" *(Tests BNS § 318 Cheating vs. § 316 Criminal breach of trust)*
* **Murder & mob lynching:** "When does culpable homicide amount to murder under BNS 2023, and what specific punishment applies when a group of five or more persons commits murder on grounds of caste or community?" *(Tests BNS §§ 100, 101, 103(1)–(2))*

**Cybercrime, UPI Fraud & Online Platforms (IT Act 2000 + BNS 2023)**

* **UPI / OTP phishing scam:** "Someone posed as a bank executive on a call, tricked me into sharing an OTP, and transferred ₹80,000 out of my account. Which provisions of the IT Act and BNS cover this offence?" *(Tests IT Act §§ 66C, 66D + BNS §§ 318, 319)*
* **Non-consensual photo morphing:** "Someone morphed my private photos and circulated them in a WhatsApp group without my consent. What cyber offences apply under the IT Act, and what rank of police officer is authorized to investigate?" *(Tests IT Act §§ 66E, 67, 67A, 78)*
* **Social media safe harbour:** "Defamatory and unlawful content targeting an individual was posted on a social media platform, and the platform ignored a government/court takedown order. Does it still enjoy intermediary immunity?" *(Tests IT Act §§ 79, 69A)*
* **Corporate data leak compensation:** "A fintech app stored customer KYC and bank details without reasonable security safeguards, and hackers leaked the database. Can affected users claim compensation?" *(Tests IT Act § 43A + DPDP Act § 8)*

**Personal Data Privacy, Spam & Minors' Data (DPDP Act 2023)**

* **Consent withdrawal & data deletion:** "I closed my account on an e-commerce app, yet the company refuses to erase my personal data and keeps sending promotional messages. What rights and remedies do I have under the DPDP Act 2023?" *(Tests DPDP §§ 6, 12, 13)*
* **Targeted ads to teenagers:** "An ed-tech platform tracks the online activity of 14-year-old students and serves targeted ads without verifiable parental consent. Which rule is violated and what is the maximum financial penalty?" *(Tests DPDP §§ 9, 33 & The Schedule)*
